Robbie Dumitru

I hold a PhD in Computer Science awarded by Adelaide University. My thesis, Hardware Security Amidst Technology Scaling, investigates fault injection and side-channel security. Previously, I earned a B.Eng. (Honours) in Electrical and Electronic Engineering, and a B.Sc. in Mathematical and Computer Sciences from The University of Adelaide.
